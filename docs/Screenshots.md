# Screenshots

The views of swarmy 0.2.4, recorded in the browser with `scripts/walkthrough/tour.py` on the 36,394
games of a `swarmy --serve` backend; the first steps in the app, Rerun and swarmy-bevy with
`scripts/walkthrough/record.sh` and 833 replays of the RSL season (0.2.1).
